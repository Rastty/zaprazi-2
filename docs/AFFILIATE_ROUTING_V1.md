# Affiliate routing v1 — Mobility

Checked: **2026-10-06**

## Principle

Recommendation and product fit are computed before merchant routing.

Affiliate commission, cookie duration, EPC or merchant preference must never change:
- solution family,
- product suitability,
- product order,
- safety gates,
- reimbursement guidance.

Until an exact reviewed affiliate destination exists, the public experience falls back to the canonical merchant product URL.

## Activation gate

To switch a direct merchant URL into an affiliate URL, require:
1. exact destination identity,
2. publisher-specific VIV link/deeplink generated by the approved account,
3. destination resolves to the intended product,
4. no unexpected cross-market redirect,
5. disclosure renders,
6. click is measurable without sending Home Advisor answers.

Until all six are true, direct links remain the safe fallback.
