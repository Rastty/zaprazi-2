## Official 2026 evidence

### SÚKL — ePoukaz

Source:
- https://sukl.gov.cz/media/tiskove-zpravy/poukaz-na-zdravotnicke-prostredky-od-1-ledna-2026-uz-jen-elektronicky/

Verified points:
- ePoukaz became the standard/povinná electronic prescription form from 1 Jan 2026, subject to statutory exceptions,
- it is a prescription / dispensing mechanism.

### SÚKL — price / reimbursement boundary

Source:
- https://sukl.gov.cz/faq/jak-epoukaz-ovlivni-ceny-ci-uhrady-zdravotnickych-prostredku/

Verified point:
- introduction of ePoukaz does not itself change prices or reimbursement amounts.

### VZP — compensatory medical devices

Source:
- https://www.vzp.cz/o-nas/tiskove-centrum/otazky-tydne/zdravotnicke-pomucky-pro-imobilni-pacienty

Page states it was processed 29 Sep 2026.

Verified high-level point:
- some compensatory-function medical devices can be reimbursed when concrete conditions are met,
- examples include selected bathing / shower / toilet aids for severely immobile patients,
- prescription and prior insurer approval can apply.

### Boundary

The broad SEO page must never imply:
- every product in a retail category is reimbursed,
- ePoukaz = automatic reimbursement,
- a retail purchase can simply be reimbursed afterward,
- the user qualifies for a specific medical-device indication.
