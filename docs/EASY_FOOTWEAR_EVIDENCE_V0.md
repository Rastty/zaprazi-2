## Safety / privacy boundary

Do not ask:
- diagnosis,
- diabetes,
- medication,
- cause of swelling,
- operation type,
- wounds.

If the user reports or implies sudden change in foot size, wound, severe pain or rapidly appearing swelling, the page should not frame footwear selection as the solution.

The engine evaluates only construction/fit workflow.
