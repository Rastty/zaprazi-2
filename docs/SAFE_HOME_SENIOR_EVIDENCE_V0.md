## Official evidence

### NZIP — fall prevention

Source:
- https://www.nzip.cz/clanek/1242-zlomenina-krcku-stehenni-kosti

Verified recommendations include:
- remove obstacles such as loose rugs/cables,
- use anti-slip measures in shower/bath,
- adequate lighting including night-time toilet routes,
- appropriate walking aids where needed.

### NZIP — preventive care for older adults

Source:
- https://www.nzip.cz/clanek/441-vseobecne-preventivni-prohlidky-dospelych

Verified 2026 context:
- preventive care for people 75–85 specifically includes fall risk among geriatric syndromes,
- the goal includes maintaining independence.
