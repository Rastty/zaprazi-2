## ZaPrazi opportunity

Do not compete only with another generic "how to choose a walker" article.

ZaPrazi's stronger differentiated intent is:
`situation → correct solution type → current acquisition path → current evidence → offer`.

The clearest first high-intent page is:
**Chodítko na pojišťovnu 2026: ePoukaz, úhrada a postup**

Why:
- combines an urgent transaction-adjacent question with current rules,
- current SÚKL evidence can be shown with a validity date,
- user can move directly from reimbursement research into the Advisor,
- commercial retail and reimbursement can be clearly separated.

## Official rule evidence used

SÚKL states:
- ePoukaz became the standard form for prescribed medical devices from 2026-01-01,
- standard ePoukaz validity is 30 days and the prescriber can extend it up to one year,
- patient access can include eRecept app, email, SMS or paper guide,
- paper prescriptions remain only for defined exceptions.

Sources:
- https://sukl.gov.cz/media/tiskove-zpravy/poukaz-na-zdravotnicke-prostredky-od-1-ledna-2026-uz-jen-elektronicky/
- https://sukl.gov.cz/faq/jaka-jsou-pravidla-pro-predpisovani-a-vydej-zdravotnickych-prostredku-od-1-1-2026/
